## Plamen function x axis matrix (patterns only)

For each in-scope function, mark the six axes:

- theft — can value leave without a matching authorization?
- liveness — can a user or the protocol get stuck?
- accounting — do tracked totals still describe reality after the call?
- provenance — is the source of a message, signature, or price authenticated?
- boundary — what trust is assumed of an external contract or token?
- identity — who is the caller, and who is allowed?

A cell is untried until it has a named check or a justified skip. Do not run or describe running the Plamen orchestrator, its CLI drivers, or its 174K-LOC runtime. Port the matrix discipline only.

## Output

Return the remainder of the matrix (function, axis, why untried) plus any boundary/provenance defect with a cited span.
